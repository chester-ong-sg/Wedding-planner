# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Millennial and Gen Z Chinese Singaporean couples planning their wedding. Digitally native, practical, and balancing the expectations of two families across multiple events (gate-crash, 过大礼 betrothal, tea ceremony, banquet). They want a tool that feels as joyful as the occasion itself — not another grey task manager. Partner is often involved. Guest lists run 80–200+ pax.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered wedding planning platform that generates a personalised timeline, checklist, and budget for Singapore couples, then helps them execute it — seating planner, milestone tracker, vendor budget tracking. Success looks like couples arriving at their wedding feeling prepared and calm, having enjoyed using the app along the way.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, warm, decisive.

**Emotional goal**: When you open the app, it should feel like a friend is helping you plan — not like filing a permit. Celebratory but grounded. The design earns the couple's trust by being clear and comprehensive, then rewards them with moments of delight.

**Cultural context**: Chinese Singaporean weddings blend Chinese tradition (auspicious red, family-first rituals, elaborate multi-event formats) with a distinctly Singapore pragmatism and modernity. The design should feel like that couple: proud of the tradition, living in 2025.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate SaaS in navy + gray**: too cold, too serious. Wedding planning is one of the most emotionally charged things couples do.
- **Western bridal aesthetic (pastel pink, serif-heavy, watercolour)**: soft, floaty, generic. Feels like The Knot. Not the energy here.
- **Kitschy traditional Chinese red-and-gold**: the garish festival aesthetic, heavy paper-cut borders. Reads as outdated, not celebratory.

## Design Principles

1. **Celebration-first, task-second**: Every interaction — completing a checklist item, hitting a milestone, confirming a budget line — should feel like a small win, not an admin chore.
2. **Family-scale clarity**: CSG weddings involve two families, large guest lists, many interdependent moving parts. Scannability and hierarchy are non-negotiable. No density traps.
3. **Culturally fluent, never costumed**: Acknowledge the rituals and milestones that matter (gate-crash, 过大礼, tea ceremony) with familiarity, not decorative Chinese motifs slapped on.
4. **Earned delight**: Motion, colour, and personality show up at the right moments — completion, milestone unlock, approaching date — not as constant noise.
5. **Warm confidence**: The brand has energy and colour. It doesn't whisper. But it earns the couple's trust through clarity and precision first, personality second.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. 4.5:1 contrast for body text, keyboard navigable, screen reader compatible. Reduced motion alternative for every animation. No colour-only information encoding.
